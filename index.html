Acorn Crew — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Acorn Crew (Android package com.goodapps.acorncrew) is a puzzle game published by GoodApps Studio (“we”). This page explains, in plain words, what the game keeps on your device, what it can send, and the choices you have.

In short

  • No account, no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device.
  • The current version has no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • Gameplay statistics and crash reports are sent to Google Firebase only if you choose “Yes, happy to”. Nothing is sent before you answer, and nothing after “No thanks”.
  • Never your name, email, contacts or advertising ID. No targeted ads. We never sell data.
  • You can change your mind at any time in Settings.

1. What stays on your device

The game saves your progress in a file in the app’s private storage: level reached, coins, boosters, stars, village, albums, outfits, lives, sound, music and vibration settings, and your answer to the statistics question together with the date you gave it (the record of your choice). It also keeps a local log of the last few hundred game events (for example, a level won), used only on the device.

We never receive this data: we have no game server. If your phone’s system backup (Google or Apple) is turned on, it may include a copy in your own account; we have no access to it.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes all of it, including your progress.

2. What is sent if you say yes (Android)

If you agree, the game uses two Google services: Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics.

  • Game events: a level started, won, failed or quit (level number, attempt, duration, boosters used, acorns left, reason for the failure, main level or event level), a booster used, coins spent (on what, and how many), a game screen opened (map, village, albums, settings…).
  • Events recorded automatically by Firebase: first launch, start of a session, time spent in the app, app update, system update, uninstall.
  • Technical information: device brand and model, Android version, app version, language, and whether the game is the store version or a test version.
  • Identifiers: random identifiers created by the app when it is installed (Analytics app instance ID, Crashlytics installation ID, Firebase installation ID). They do not say who you are, and they change if you reinstall the game or clear its data.
  • Approximate location: country and region, estimated by Google from your IP address. For users in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics does not log or store the IP address.
  • Crash reports: the technical trace of the crash, technical information about the device and the app at that moment, and the level and screen you were on.

Never collected: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files, precise location, advertising ID, Android ID, payment details, or anything you type.

Why: to fix bugs and crashes, to tune how hard the levels are, and to see which parts of the game players enjoy. This data is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

On iOS: the current iOS version sends nothing, even after “Yes”. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

3. Before you answer, and after “No thanks”

  • The game starts with all collection switched off.
  • Until you answer, nothing leaves your device. Events from the current session wait in memory only (never on disk) and are discarded unless you say yes during that session. A crash report recorded before your answer is deleted at the next launch, and will not be sent even if you say yes later.
  • If you choose “No thanks” (on the question card, or later in Settings), all collection stops at once, crash reports still waiting on the device are deleted, and nothing more is kept.
  • Closing the card without answering decides nothing; the game will ask again in a later session.

4. Change your mind at any time

On the home screen, tap the gear (Settings), then the line “Statistics: on” or “Statistics: off”. The question opens again: choose “No thanks” or “Yes, happy to”. Your new choice applies immediately, and nothing in the game changes either way.

5. How long data is kept

  • Analytics: detailed event data is kept for 2 months, then deleted. Aggregated reports (totals such as “number of players who reached level 10”) contain no identifier and may be kept longer.
  • Crashlytics: crash reports and their identifiers are kept for 90 days.

Saying no stops any new data; what was sent before follows these periods. Because this data contains no name or contact details, we usually cannot tell which data is yours. If you would like us to try to delete it, write to us (see Contact).

6. Who processes the data, and where

Google processes this data on our behalf, as a service provider (“data processor”), under the Firebase terms and their data processing and security terms. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and may be processed in any country where Google has facilities, with the safeguards set out in those terms. We have switched off advertising signals: no advertising ID is collected, and the ad-related consent signals are always sent as refused. We do not share this data with anyone else.

More: Privacy and Security in Firebase · Google Privacy Policy

7. Ads and purchases

The current version has no ads and no in-app purchases. If we add them, this page will be updated before that version is released, and where the law requires it you will be asked for your consent again.

8. Permissions

The game does not access your contacts, files, photos, location, microphone or camera.

9. Children

Acorn Crew is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game never asks for an age or a name. If a child plays, a parent can choose “No thanks” (or switch Statistics off in Settings): nothing is sent. In some countries, a parent’s consent is needed to say yes below the age of 16 (or a lower age set by local law). If you believe a child’s data has been sent, write to us.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings (see section 4), without losing anything in the game. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

1. Was auf deinem Gerät bleibt

Das Spiel speichert deinen Fortschritt in einer Datei im privaten Speicher der App: erreichtes Level, Münzen, Booster, Sterne, Dorf, Alben, Garderobe, Leben, Einstellungen für Ton, Musik und Vibration sowie deine Antwort auf die Statistik-Frage zusammen mit dem Tag, an dem du sie gegeben hast (der Nachweis deiner Entscheidung). Außerdem führt es ein lokales Protokoll der letzten paar hundert Spielereignisse (zum Beispiel ein gewonnenes Level), das nur auf dem Gerät verwendet wird.

Diese Daten erreichen uns nie: Wir haben keinen Spielserver. Wenn die Systemsicherung deines Telefons (Google oder Apple) eingeschaltet ist, kann sie eine Kopie in deinem eigenen Konto ablegen; darauf haben wir keinen Zugriff.

Wenn du das Spiel deinstallierst oder seine Daten löschst, wird alles entfernt, auch dein Fortschritt.

2. Was gesendet wird, wenn du zustimmst (Android)

Mit deiner Zustimmung nutzt das Spiel zwei Dienste von Google: Google Analytics für Firebase und Firebase Crashlytics.

  • Spielereignisse: ein Level begonnen, gewonnen, verloren oder abgebrochen (Levelnummer, Versuch, Dauer, eingesetzte Booster, übrige Eicheln, Grund für das Scheitern, Haupt- oder Event-Level), ein eingesetzter Booster, ausgegebene Münzen (wofür und wie viele), ein geöffneter Spielbildschirm (Karte, Dorf, Alben, Einstellungen …).
  • Ereignisse, die Firebase automatisch erfasst: erster Start, Beginn einer Sitzung, Nutzungsdauer der App, App-Update, System-Update, Deinstallation.
  • Technische Angaben: Marke und Modell des Geräts, Android-Version, App-Version, Sprache und ob es sich um die Store-Version oder eine Testversion handelt.
  • Kennungen: zufällige Kennungen, die die App bei der Installation erzeugt (App-Instanz-ID von Analytics, Installations-ID von Crashlytics, Firebase-Installations-ID). Sie verraten nicht, wer du bist, und ändern sich, wenn du das Spiel neu installierst oder seine Daten löschst.

  • Absturzberichte: die technische Spur des Absturzes, technische Angaben zu Gerät und App in diesem Moment sowie das Level und der Bildschirm, auf dem du warst.

Nie erfasst: dein Name, deine E-Mail-Adresse, deine Telefonnummer, deine Kontakte, Fotos oder Dateien, dein genauer Standort, die Werbe-ID, die Android-ID, Zahlungsdaten oder irgendetwas, das du eintippst.

Wozu: um Fehler und Abstürze zu beheben, die Schwierigkeit der Level abzustimmen und zu sehen, welche Teile des Spiels gut ankommen. Die Daten werden weder für Werbung noch für Profilbildung genutzt und nie verkauft. Rechtsgrundlage: deine Einwilligung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO).

Unter iOS: Die aktuelle iOS-Version sendet nichts, auch nicht nach „Ja“. Sollte sich das ändern, passen wir diese Seite vorher an.

  • Das Spiel startet mit vollständig ausgeschalteter Datenerfassung.
  • Solange du nicht geantwortet hast, verlässt nichts dein Gerät. Die Ereignisse der Sitzung warten nur im Arbeitsspeicher (nie auf dem Speicher des Geräts) und verfallen, wenn du in dieser Sitzung nicht zustimmst. Ein Absturzbericht, der vor deiner Antwort entstanden ist, wird beim nächsten Start gelöscht und auch dann nicht gesendet, wenn du später zustimmst.
  • Wählst du „Nein, danke“ (auf der Frage-Karte oder später in den Einstellungen), stoppt jede Erfassung sofort, noch wartende Absturzberichte auf dem Gerät werden gelöscht und nichts weiter wird aufbewahrt.

동의하시면 게임은 Google의 두 가지 서비스, Google Analytics for Firebase와 Firebase Crashlytics를 사용합니다.

  • 게임 이벤트: 레벨 시작·클리어·실패·중단(레벨 번호, 도전 횟수, 걸린 시간, 사용한 부스터, 남은 도토리 수, 실패 원인, 본편 레벨인지 이벤트 레벨인지), 부스터 사용, 코인 사용(어디에 몇 개), 게임 화면 열기(지도, 마을, 앨범, 설정 등).
  • Firebase가 자동으로 기록하는 이벤트: 첫 실행, 세션 시작, 앱 사용 시간, 앱 업데이트, 운영체제 업데이트, 앱 삭제.
  • 기술 정보: 기기 제조사와 모델, Android 버전, 앱 버전, 언어, 스토어 버전인지 테스트 버전인지 여부.
  • 식별자: 설치할 때 앱이 만드는 무작위 ID(Analytics 앱 인스턴스 ID, Crashlytics 설치 ID, Firebase 설치 ID). 여러분이 누구인지 알려 주지 않으며, 게임을 다시 설치하거나 데이터를 지우면 새로 바뀝니다.
  • 대략적인 위치: Google이 IP 주소로 추정하는 국가와 지역. EU, 영국, 스위스 사용자의 경우 Google 애널리틱스는 IP 주소를 기록하거나 저장하지 않습니다.
  • 오류 보고서: 오류의 기술적 기록, 그 순간의 기기와 앱에 관한 기술 정보, 플레이 중이던 레벨과 화면.

수집하지 않는 정보: 이름, 이메일 주소, 전화번호, 연락처, 사진이나 파일, 정확한 위치, 광고 ID, Android ID, 결제 정보, 여러분이 입력하는 모든 내용.

이용 목적: 버그와 오류를 고치고, 레벨 난이도를 맞추고, 게임의 어떤 부분을 즐기시는지 알기 위해서입니다. 광고나 프로파일링에 쓰지 않으며, 판매하지 않습니다. 처리 근거: 여러분의 동의(EU 일반 개인정보 보호법(GDPR) 제6조 제1항 (a)).

iOS: 현재 iOS 버전은 ‘네’를 선택해도 아무것도 전송하지 않습니다. 이것이 바뀔 경우 먼저 이 페이지를 수정합니다.

3. 답하기 전, 그리고 ‘아니요’를 선택한 뒤

  • 게임은 모든 수집이 꺼진 상태로 시작합니다.
  • 답하시기 전까지는 아무것도 기기 밖으로 나가지 않습니다. 해당 세션의 이벤트는 메모리에서만 대기하며(저장 공간에는 쓰지 않습니다), 그 세션 동안 ‘네’를 선택하지 않으면 폐기됩니다. 답하기 전에 기록된 오류 보고서는 다음 실행 때 삭제되며, 나중에 ‘네’를 선택해도 전송되지 않습니다.
  • ‘아니요’를 선택하면(질문 카드에서든, 나중에 설정에서든) 모든 수집이 즉시 멈추고, 기기에 남아 있던 미전송 오류 보고서는 삭제되며, 그 뒤로는 아무것도 보관하지 않습니다.
  • 답하지 않고 카드를 닫으면 아무것도 결정되지 않습니다. 다음 세션에서 다시 여쭤봅니다.

4. 언제든지 선택을 바꿀 수 있습니다

홈 화면에서 톱니바퀴(설정)를 누른 다음 ‘통계: 켬’ 또는 ‘통계: 끔’ 줄을 누르세요. 질문이 다시 열리면 ‘아니요’ 또는 ‘네, 좋아요’를 선택하시면 됩니다. 새 선택은 바로 적용되며, 어느 쪽을 선택해도 게임 내용은 달라지지 않습니다.

5. 보관 기간

  • Analytics: 세부 이벤트 데이터는 2개월 동안 보관된 뒤 삭제됩니다. 집계 보고서(‘레벨 10에 도달한 플레이어 수’ 같은 합계)에는 식별자가 없으며 더 오래 보관될 수 있습니다.
  • Crashlytics: 오류 보고서와 그 식별자는 90일 동안 보관됩니다.

‘아니요’를 선택하면 새로운 전송은 모두 멈추지만, 그 전에 전송된 데이터는 위 기간에 따릅니다. 이 데이터에는 이름이나 연락처가 없어서, 저희는 대개 어느 데이터가 여러분의 것인지 알 수 없습니다. 삭제를 원하시면 연락해 주세요. 최대한 도와드리겠습니다.

6. 처리 위탁과 국외 이전

Google은 Firebase 이용약관과 그에 딸린 데이터 처리 및 보안 약관에 따라, 저희의 수탁자로서 이 데이터를 처리합니다. 데이터는 전송 중 암호화되며(HTTPS), 동의 후 게임을 하는 동안 네트워크를 통해 전송되어 한국 밖을 포함해 Google이 시설을 둔 국가에서 처리될 수 있습니다. 이 경우에도 위 약관에 정한 보호 조치가 적용됩니다. 광고 관련 신호는 꺼 두었습니다. 광고 ID는 수집하지 않으며, 광고 관련 동의 신호는 항상 ‘거부’로 보냅니다. 저희는 이 데이터를 다른 누구와도 공유하지 않습니다.
